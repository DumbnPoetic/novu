Who am I?

I’m a 18-year-old developer currently living in Philadelphia, who first dabbled with Cheat Engine at 7 years of age. After a little bit of research, I found myself on UnknownCheats and haven’t looked back since!

When I turned 10, I realized that Java was fucking epic! I did a lot of other stuff besides that, like learning the basics of C/C++, but I would always find myself coming back to Java. When I was 11, I created my first shit Minecraft memory modification client (cheats); however, that one project would fuel my passion for years to come. Just 2 years later, I made my first CS:GO memory modification menu called LanaDelReyHook. Though it was never released publicly, it’s still my favorite project to date!

Nowadays, I focus on the theory/math behind computer science rather than sitting down and coding. #ILoveLLMs
